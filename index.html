What we do

Everything your board and examiner ask for, done for you

1. Your data

We collect it

Send the reports your banking system already produces. We put them together, so your team does not have to.

2. Your risk

We measure it

How your yearly earnings and the value of your capital would change if rates rose or fell, including every scenario your examiner expects to see.

3. Your investments

We value them

Every bond and mortgage-backed investment priced and checked against real market trades.

4. Your options

We show you the fix

If you are outside your limits, a short list of practical steps using what you already own, with the cost of each, so the board can decide.

5. Optional

If you are allowed to use interest-rate hedges, we size them to what is left and prepare all the accounting paperwork.

6. Every quarter

We check ourselves

Why trust the numbers

We test our forecasts against what really happened

Most providers ask you to take their assumptions on faith. We checked ours against years of real results from credit unions we had not studied, and we publish the outcome.

How it works

From your reports to a board-ready answer in one quarter

  1. You send your reports

    Loan and savings reports from your banking system, your investment statement and your current policy limits.

  2. We check everything ties out

    Our totals match your financial statements before we report a single number.

  3. You get a board pack

    What a rate move would do, where you stand against your limits, and the plan if action is needed, in plain English.

  4. We follow up every quarter

    We compare last quarter's forecast with your actual results, ready for your examiner's file.
